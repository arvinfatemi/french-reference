The three loans worth reciting

① shared stem futur & conditionnel — always, irregulars included (ser-, aur-, ir-, voudr-)

② shared endings imparfait & conditionnel — -ais -ais -ait -ions -iez -aient

③ futur endings = avoir présent: ai, as, a, (av)ons, (av)ez, ont

G · Lookalike spotter

The four lookalikes of parler — only stem & one vowel differ

passé simple 📚 je parlai

imparfait je parlais

futur je parlerai

conditionnel je parlerais

Hear an r before the ending → future family. Ending in -s → “would/was” family (imparfait or conditionnel).

Identical twins — forms that are the same on purpose

imparfait = cond. same endings (-ais…) — only the stem tells them apart: parlais vs parlerais

futur = cond. same stem (always in -r) — only the endings differ: -ai vs -ais

subj. = présent for -er verbs: je/tu/il/ils forms are identical (que je parle)

subj. = imparfait nous/vous forms are identical for almost all verbs (que nous parlions)

futur endings = present tense of avoir: -ai -as -a -ons -ez -ont
